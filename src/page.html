Welcome

This tool finds random official Google Street View locations that match what you choose, and you import the result into map-making.app.

  1. Choose where: one or more countries, a continent or the whole world. With several countries, the locations are shared between them like in a well-known GeoGuessr map of your choice.
  2. Choose what to look for: spots where Street View shows no road name, spots where the coverage ends (only one arrow), photos from certain years, or a mix.
  3. Choose how many, then select "Find locations".
  4. Download the map file when the search is done, or stop it and download what was found.
  5. Run a search again later from "History": your last 20 searches are kept in this browser.
Large searches take a long time. Every location is found by trying random spots until one matches. Dead ends are rare, so thousands of them can take hours. You can stop at any time and keep what was found. If you close the tab, the search is kept: you can continue it next time.

History

Your last searches, kept in this browser only. Only the settings are kept, not the results: running a search again gives a new random selection.
