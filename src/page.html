Welcome

This tool finds random official Google Street View locations that match what you choose, and you import the result into map-making.app.

  1. Choose a country: start typing its name and pick it from the list.
  2. Choose what to look for: spots where Street View shows no road name, spots where the coverage ends (only one arrow), or both.
  3. Choose how many, then select "Find locations".
  4. Download the map file when the search is done, or stop it and download what was found.
Large searches take a long time. Every location is found by trying random spots until one matches. Dead ends are rare, so thousands of them can take hours. You can stop at any time and keep what was found.
