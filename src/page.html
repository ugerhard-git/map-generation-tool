Welcome

This tool finds random official Google Street View locations that match what you choose, and you import the result into map-making.app.

  1. Choose where: one or more countries, a continent or the whole world. With several countries, the locations are shared between them like in a well-known GeoGuessr map of your choice.
  2. Or share them your own way, under "Distribution": make your own from one of those maps, or start from scratch with equal shares for every country (or set them all to 0 and enter only the countries you want). Give each country a percentage or an exact number of locations, and save it as a preset in this browser.
  3. Choose what to look for, if you like, in the sections of step 2: spots where Street View shows no road name, spots where the coverage ends (only one arrow), intersections (three or more arrows), spots with no arrows at all, photos from a certain period or camera, or a mix. "Camera view" sets where the camera faces, how far it tilts and how far it is zoomed in.
  4. Choose how many, then select "Find locations".
  5. Download the map file when the search is done, or stop it and download what was found. The map on this page shows the first 20,000 locations, to stay quick; the map file has all of them.
  6. Run a search again later from "History": your last 20 searches are kept in this browser.
  7. Keep or share your settings with "Settings file": save every setting, your own distribution included, to a file, and load it again later, in another browser or on another computer, or send it to someone. Your Google key is never in it.
Large searches take a long time. Every location is found by trying random spots until one matches. Dead ends are rare, so thousands of them can take hours. You can stop at any time and keep what was found, then continue the search later, as long as you keep its settings. If you close the tab, the search is kept: you can continue it next time.
