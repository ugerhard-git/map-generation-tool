Welcome

This tool finds random unofficial Google Street View locations: everything Google shows that Google did not take itself, such as photospheres, drone shots and road coverage taken with other cameras. You import the result into map-making.app.

  1. Choose where: one or more countries or territories, a continent or the whole world. With several, the locations are shared between them by their land area, or in equal shares.
  2. Or share them your own way, under "Distribution": give each country a percentage or an exact number of locations, and save it as a preset in this browser.
  3. Choose what to look for, if you like, in the sections of step 2: photos from a certain period, whole 360° photos only, large images only, or photos by certain uploaders (or not by them).
  4. Choose how many, then select "Find locations".
  5. Download the map file when the search is done, or stop it and download what was found. The map on this page shows the first 20,000 locations, to stay quick; the map file has all of them.
  6. Run a search again later from "History", or keep your settings in a "Settings file", as in the Map Generation Tool. Your Google key is never in either.
Unofficial coverage is hard to find. Google's service can be asked for its own coverage only, but not for unofficial coverage only. So every random spot is looked up, and where Google's own coverage is nearer, it answers with that, and any unofficial photo next to it stays hidden. Most locations are found away from Google's roads, and a search takes far longer than one for official coverage. Indoor photos are found too: Google does not say which photospheres are indoors. You can stop at any time and keep what was found, and continue later.
